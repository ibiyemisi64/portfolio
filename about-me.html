IBIYEMISI GBENEBOR

I design dream user experiences - from infrastructure to interfaces.

I'm currently pursuing an M.S. in Computer Science at Brown University, after spending six years in industry as a software and systems engineer. I returned to graduate school because I'm passionate about leading innovation and creating inclusive, community-centered technology. My journey began in graphic design, and I’ve always been drawn to the intersection of creativity and impact — from leading local service projects to building systems that make technology more accessible. In my professional career, I've developed full-scale products, designed and deployed cloud infrastructure, and created APIs and applications that power real-world use cases. Through my graduate studies, I’ve deepened my ability to design and develop systems through a socio-technical lens — one that blends user empathy with robust engineering. As I continue to grow, I see myself transforming — like a butterfly — into a collaborative team lead who can take products from spark to launch, with interaction design and accessibility at the core
